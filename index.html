CHECKPOINT 02 // RELAY CONCEPT

WHAT IS
CODE RELAY?

Code Relay is a team-based programming challenge where three members combine technical knowledge, logical thinking, debugging ability and problem-solving skills to progress through multiple challenges.

01

TEAMWORK

Work together, combine individual strengths and keep the team moving forward.

02

SPEED & PRECISION

Think quickly, make accurate decisions and perform under time pressure.

03

PROBLEM SOLVING

Analyse challenges, identify problems and develop effective solutions.

CHECKPOINT 03 // COMPETITION PROTOCOL

GENERAL RULES

Follow competition protocol for fair and seamless execution.

01

TEAM COMPOSITION

Each team must consist of exactly 3 members. Members may be from different colleges, and the same members must remain throughout all rounds.

02

TEAM ID

Each team will be assigned a unique Team ID, which must be used throughout the event for identification and submissions.

03

REPORTING & CONDUCT

Participants must report before the scheduled time and follow the instructions of the event coordinators, judges, and technical team. Professional and respectful conduct is expected throughout the event.

04

PERMITTED RESOURCES

Participants may use only the resources, devices, software, and environment permitted for each round. Unauthorized websites, applications, external assistance, or resources are prohibited.

05

FAIR PLAY

Sharing or copying code, answers, solutions, or receiving assistance from other teams, spectators, or unauthorized sources is strictly prohibited. Any attempt to manipulate the platform, timer, scores, or submissions may lead to disqualification.

06

SUBMISSIONS & TECHNICAL ISSUES

All submissions must be completed within the specified time limit. Late submissions will not be considered. Genuine technical issues must be reported immediately to the technical team.

07

SCORING & DECISIONS

Official submissions, timestamps, and system records will be used for scoring and verification. The decision of the authorized judging/event committee regarding scoring, qualification, and disqualification will be final.

08
DISTRIBUTION

PRIZE DISTRIBUTION

Prize distribution will depend on the number of registered teams:

Fewer than 15 teams: First Place only
20+ teams: First & Second Place
30+ teams: First, Second & Third Place

The organizing committee may make necessary changes to the schedule or competition procedure due to technical or logistical circumstances.
